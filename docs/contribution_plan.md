# Contribution Plan

This is a 3-member Track B structure. Update names and student IDs before submission.

## Member 1: Scope Router and Evaluation Lead

- Designs H1 scope and question-type router.
- Owns evaluation set construction and scoring consistency.
- Analyzes router ablation and out-of-scope/refusal cases.

## Member 2: Retrieval Harness Lead

- Designs H2 evidence retrieval.
- Owns document ingestion, chunking, and retrieval hit analysis.
- Analyzes top-k ablation and citation hit rate.

## Member 3: Verifier Harness Lead

- Designs H3 misconception and faithfulness verifier.
- Owns false-premise cases and faithfulness scoring.
- Analyzes verifier ablation, unsupported claims, and cost/latency tradeoff.

## Shared Extension: Formula Guard

- Designs H4 deterministic calculation/formula guard.
- Owns calculation/formula cases and the A4 ablation.
- Analyzes arithmetic and formula-substitution failures, especially CNN shape and parameter-count cases.

## Shared Work

- Report writing
- Slides
- Presentation Q&A
- Manual scoring sanity check
- AI-tool disclosure
